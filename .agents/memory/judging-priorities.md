name: Hackathon judging priorities
description: Product intent and standing constraints for the employer extension.
---

HR, employers and Heads of Department are a primary target audience alongside individual workers. Preserve the individual experience when extending employer workflows.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested both audiences for the Domain 1 Aiversecity Future-Ready Workforce hackathon.

**How to apply:** Judge-facing workflows should be immediately explorable: synthetic autofill for surveys, useful seeded department analysis on first load, and a short route from a shared survey link to employee value and HR planning. Do not substitute presentation-only demonstrations for the working submission-to-dashboard flow.

The employer entry should introduce the complete workflow on a landing page before opening a smooth, simple, slide-by-slide functional demo. Every feature should explain what it is used for.

**Why:** The user asked to use the individual workspace's presentation quality as the reference rather than opening HR directly into a dense dashboard.

**How to apply:** Keep the introduction separate from the hands-on workflow, preserve real operations in each chapter, and retain department/campaign context between steps.
